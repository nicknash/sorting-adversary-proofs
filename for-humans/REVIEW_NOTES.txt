Exposition
----------
The four-sentence approved abstract is unchanged. The methodological disclosure
remains immediately after it, identifies GPT 6 Astra, and states that the text
except the abstract is AI-generated and the author is preparing a clear,
human-readable exposition. The author is Nicholas Nash, with the supplied ORCID.

The algorithm and all repair parameters now precede the proofs. Legality and
graph bounds are organized as an induction; anchor selection, packing, and the
single potential supply the comparison bound. Complete proofs of the density,
locality, and exact enumeration lemmas are in the appendices. Historical citations
include Atallah--Kosaraju, Borodin--Guibas--Lynch--Yao, Richards--Vaidya,
Brodal--Chaudhuri--Radhakrishnan, and Kaplan--Zamir--Zwick. Three TikZ figures
show early separation versus retained arrows, refinement, and geometric packing.

The exact fixed-parameter lower bound is retained; the redundant conservative
numerical expansion of its denominator is replaced by its asymptotic estimate.
The source manuscript's development history, finite-check discussion, and
unrelated counting-scheme discussion are omitted from the paper.
