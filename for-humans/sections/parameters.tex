\section{Parameter reference}\label{app:parameters}

Table~\ref{tab:parameters} collects the parameters of the adversary. The last two columns
distinguish where a parameter's role is introduced from where its value
is chosen and explained. All choices are fixed before the first query.
The constants used to bound the estimator's neighborhood are given in
\eqref{eq:local-moment} and~\eqref{eq:local-radius}; its size is bounded by
\eqref{eq:local-size}.
