\section{Parameter reference}\label{app:parameters}

Table~\ref{tab:parameters} collects the parameters of the adversary and
the coefficients used in its comparison bound. The last two columns
distinguish where a parameter's role is introduced from where its value
is chosen and explained. All choices are fixed before the first query.
The constants for the finite estimator are given separately in
\eqref{eq:finite-moment}--\eqref{eq:finite-size}.
