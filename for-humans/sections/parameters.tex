\section{Parameter reference}\label{app:parameters}

Table~\ref{tab:parameters} collects the parameters of the adversary and its
analysis. The last two columns
distinguish where a parameter's role is introduced from where its value
is chosen and explained. All choices are fixed before the first query.
Appendix~\ref{app:coupling} gives explicit choices for the digit alphabet,
number of sweeps, and neighborhood radius in the local enclosure theorem.
