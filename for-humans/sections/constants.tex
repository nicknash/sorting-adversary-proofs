\section{The explicit comparison coefficient}\label{app:constants}

We verify the choices of $a,b$ in Lemma~\ref{lem:refinement-cost} and the
coefficient claimed in Theorem~\ref{thm:fixed-stage}. Write $j=\log k$,
so $j$ is an integer at least six. Since
\[
-\log(1-1/k)\le\frac1{(k-1)\ln2}\le\frac3{2k},
\]
we may take
\begin{equation}\label{eq:explicit-affine-coefficients}
a=\frac{1+3/(2k)}{1-1/k}
 =1+\frac5{2(k-1)},\qquad b=4a+j+1.
\end{equation}
These choices satisfy $a\ge\gamma/(1-1/k)$ as required by the refinement
proof. They also give $a\ge1$ and $b\ge1$.

Put
\[
D=k^2-(j+5)k+j-6.
\]
The inequality $k\ge j+6$ gives $D>0$, and
$k+1-b=D/(k-1)>0$. Hence $b<k+1$, and the coefficients
\eqref{eq:charge-coefficient} and~\eqref{eq:degree-allowance} become
\[
\lambda=\frac{(k+1)(2k+3)}{2D},\qquad
\sigma=j+1+4\lambda.
\]
Using $a_\eta\le1+1/(2k)$ from \eqref{eq:comparison-loss}, direct
simplification gives
\begin{align}
1+\frac{2j+17}{k}
 -\left(\lambda\left(1+\frac1{2k}\right)+\frac\sigma k\right)
&=\frac{P}{4kD},\label{eq:comparison-charge}\\
P&=16k^2-(4j^2+80j+395)k+4j^2+40j-411.\nonumber
\end{align}
For $j=6$ and $k=64$, $P=293>0$.
For $j\ge7$, the inequality
\[
4j^2+80j+395\le16\cdot2^j=16k
\]
holds at $j=7$ and persists as $j$ increases: the polynomial on the
left grows by less than a factor of two at each step. Also
$4j^2+40j-411>0$ in this range. Thus $P>0$, proving
\[
\lambda a_\eta+\frac\sigma k\le1+\frac{2\log k+17}{k}.
\]
Finally,
\[
\log(n!)\ge\int_1^n\log x\,dx
=n\log n-\frac n{\ln2}+\frac1{\ln2},
\]
and $1+1/\ln2<3$ gives $\log(n!)-n\ge n\log n-3n$.
Substitution into \eqref{eq:comparison-total} at termination yields
\eqref{eq:exact-bound}.
